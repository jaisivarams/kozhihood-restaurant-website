OUR HERITAGE

Good Food. Bold Flavours.
Made to Crave.

Kozhihood is a food destination in the heart of Erode, bringing together satisfying biryanis, flavour-packed fried rice and noodles, and delicious chicken and vegetarian starters.

From comforting classics to bold, indulgent favourites, our menu is made for those who love food with character. Whether you're craving a hearty biryani, a sizzling plate of fried rice, or something crispy and spicy to share, Kozhihood has something worth coming back for.

Located at Divya Complex on Park Road, near IOB Bank and opposite Ellai Mariyamman Kovil, Kozhihood is here to make every meal simple, satisfying, and full of flavour.

Come hungry. Leave satisfied.
